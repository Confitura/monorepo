# Facts and editions

Use these facts when writing copy, so every page tells the same story. Update the table after each edition.

## The conference

- **Name:** Confitura. It was held as *Javarsovia* until 2010 and has been Confitura since 2011.
- **What:** one of Poland's premier and longest-running software conferences, and its largest Java community conference, about the JVM and its ecosystem. It is non-profit, run by a team of Java developers, architects and industry professionals (more than 30 people in 2023). It started in 2007.
- **Goal, in the sites' words:** "Our main goal is to promote JVM technologies and have fun doing so."
- **Pitch lines the sites use:** a low attending fee that makes Confitura accessible for everyone, with money from sales going to charity (2023); "experienced experts and industry rockstars" alongside new speakers "just starting their journey" (2026); content in both Polish and English.
- **What attendees get:** parallel tracks, workshops, an expo area, networking in the breaks and an afterparty, historically called **SPOINA**. The 2023 site also had a lean coffee session.
- **Contact:** confitura@confitura.pl · confitura.pl · newsletter.confitura.pl
- **Social:** X @confiturapl · facebook.com/Confitura · linkedin.com/company/confitura · youtube.com/confiturapl

## 2026 edition

- **Dates:** 25–26 September 2026 (Friday and Saturday).
- **Venue:** ADN Conference Center, Browary Warszawskie, ul. Grzybowska 56, Warsaw.
- **Tickets:** Standard (2 days), Student (2 days), One-day standard, One-day student. All include talks, panels, the expo area and one workshop. None include lunch. Student tickets need a valid student ID.
- **Partner tiers:** platinum, gold, silver, bronze, tech, media.
- **Site:** confitura.pl (Nuxt). Hero: red and green binary rain on black. After the event: "Thank you! And see you next time!" with the 2025 aftermovie.
