With a strong entrepreneurial culture and a long history of innovation, Cantor combines deep industry expertise with technology to build solutions for clients around the world. The firm operates globally and brings together thousands of professionals across finance, technology, and related industries.

At Cantor Fitzgerald, innovation is not only about financial markets - it is also about continuously developing the technology, systems, and infrastructure that power a global financial institution.
